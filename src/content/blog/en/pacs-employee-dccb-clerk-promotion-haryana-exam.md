Many employees working in PACS think that becoming a clerk in a DCCB (District Central Cooperative Bank) or HARCO Bank is their natural "promotion". In reality there are two separate routes — one is **open direct recruitment**, and the other is a **limited promotion quota**, which was approved only for Class IV employees of PACS. This article explains both separately.

> ⚠️ **Information checked up to 18 October 2026.** The recruitment points have been matched with HARCO Bank's Advertisement No. 8/2019, and the promotion quota point with the Haryana Government's press release of 16 November 2022 (see "Sources" below). We have not seen any clerk recruitment advertisement after 2019, nor the amendment to the service rules that puts the promotion quota into effect. Always take the conditions of your recruitment from the official advertisement of that same year.

## Route 1 — Direct Recruitment

HARCO Bank's Advertisement No. 8/2019 (14.08.2019) invited applications for **"direct recruitment"** to the posts of Clerk, Junior Accountant, Senior Accountant and Assistant Manager in **Haryana State Cooperative Apex Bank (HARCO Bank) and the state's 18 District Central Cooperative Banks**. The main points of that advertisement:

- Applications online on HARCO Bank's website; the **online exam was conducted by IBPS** (Institute of Banking Personnel Selection).
- Selection as per the **Common Cadre Rules** (1988 and 1975) for employees of HARCO Bank and the District Central Cooperative Banks.
- Preliminary and main exams for clerk; there was **no interview for the clerk post**.
- Qualification for clerk: graduation with 55% marks, or commerce graduation with 50% marks.
- Age: 18 to 42 years (on the last date of application).
- After appointment, passing HARTRON's SETC (computer) test was required, unless the candidate had a qualification exempting from it.

So this is an open competitive exam, in which any eligible candidate can apply — PACS employees too.

## What relaxation a PACS employee gets in this recruitment

As per the 2019 advertisement, the "competent authority" can give **employees of government/boards/corporations/cooperative institutions** a relaxation of **up to 10 years** in the age limit. Note — the advertisement says "may be relaxed", meaning this relaxation is not automatic; it comes with the approval of the competent authority. For this, get a service certificate from your employer made in good time.

> 💡 The 2019 advertisement also had marks for "socio-economic criteria and experience" for experience in a Haryana Government department/board/corporation. Whether PACS experience came under that definition is not clear from the advertisement, and we have not checked whether these marks stayed in later recruitments — so do not rely on this.

## Route 2 — Promotion quota for Class IV employees of PACS

As per the Haryana Government's press release of **16 November 2022**, the standing committee of the Haryana Bureau of Public Enterprises approved the agenda to give promotion to **Class IV employees** of DCCBs and PACS:

- In the District Central Cooperative Banks, **70% of clerk posts are filled by direct recruitment** (this provision already existed).
- The remaining **30% of posts by promotion** — of these, **20% for Class IV employees of DCCBs** and **10% for Class IV employees of PACS**.

So a limited route of promotion up to DCCB clerk has been approved for **Class IV (fourth-grade)** employees of PACS. But it is not for other PACS posts such as clerk/secretary, and the press release does not say under which rule, and on what conditions (length of service, qualification, seniority), this quota came into effect. For this, ask your DCCB or RCS office for the amendment to the service rules and check it.

## Document preparation — a small but important point

At the document verification stage of selection, many good candidates get stuck only because of incomplete or wrong documents. So keep these ready from the start — educational certificates, proof of age, category certificate (where applicable), and proof of current cooperative service (for claiming age relaxation or promotion). The spelling of your name should be the same in every document.

## The difference between PACS and DCCB service arrangements

The service arrangements of PACS employees and the recruitment of DCCB/HARCO Bank run under separate frameworks. Chapter V of the Haryana Act (Sections 37-38) provides for a "common cadre" (see [PACS service rules](/blog/pacs-service-rules-haryana-recruitment-promotion)), while employees of HARCO Bank and the District Central Cooperative Banks have their own Common Cadre Rules (1988 and 1975), under which the recruitment above takes place. In its advertisement, HARCO Bank describes itself as "A Scheduled Bank".

## Common misunderstandings

- *"PACS experience guarantees a seat."* — No; in direct recruitment, experience can only get you age relaxation (with the approval of the competent authority); the seat comes on merit.
- *"Every PACS employee has a promotion quota in the DCCB."* — No; the 10% quota approved in 2022 is only for **Class IV** employees of PACS.
- *"There is no quota at all."* — This is also wrong; the government's 2022 press release says a 10% quota was approved.
- *"This exam will be easy."* — No; direct recruitment is a full competitive exam.

## Practical preparation tips

In the 2019 advertisement, the clerk preliminary exam had 30-30 questions each on English language, reasoning and numerical ability (90 minutes). Take the pattern of a new recruitment from its own advertisement. Usually these subjects come in such exams:

- **General knowledge and current affairs** (including Haryana-focused)
- **Mathematics/numerical aptitude**
- **Reasoning**
- **Hindi and English language**
- **Basic computer knowledge**

A candidate working in a PACS has the edge of practical experience of cooperative banking, but in the written exam this helps only when there is solid preparation of the basic subjects.

## Stages of the selection process — as per the 2019 advertisement

1. **Online application** — check eligibility, then apply and pay the fee.
2. **Preliminary exam** (for clerk) — online, by IBPS.
3. **Main exam** — the merit is made here.
4. **Document verification** — educational, age, category and (where applicable) service proof.
5. **Final merit and appointment** — no interview for clerk (there was an interview for Assistant Manager).

## Example of a candidate

Suppose Sunita, a Junior Assistant in a PACS, wants to become a DCCB clerk. She is not a Class IV employee, so the 2022 promotion quota does not apply to her. Her age is a little above the normal limit; being an employee of a cooperative institution, she can ask the competent authority for age relaxation. If it is approved she will be eligible for the exam, but she will get a seat on the same merit as any new candidate. *(This is an imaginary example.)*

On the other hand, for a Class IV employee of the same PACS (such as a peon), the route of the 10% promotion quota may also be open — provided the conditions of the service rules are met.

## Practical advice

1. First see which category you are in — if you are a Class IV employee, ask the DCCB/RCS about the rules of the promotion quota.
2. For everyone else, direct recruitment is the only route — prepare for it seriously like an independent exam.
3. Get the service certificate for age relaxation made in good time.
4. In every recruitment, take the qualification, age, fee and selection process from the official advertisement of that same year.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can a PACS clerk directly become a DCCB clerk?** — Through direct recruitment, yes — but by passing an open competitive exam. He can get age relaxation of up to 10 years from the competent authority (2019 advertisement).
**Do PACS employees have any promotion quota in the DCCB?** — As per the Haryana Government's press release of 16.11.2022, approval was given to fill 10% of DCCB clerk posts by promotion of **Class IV** employees of PACS. That press release has no such quota for PACS employees in other posts.
**Who does recruitment in DCCB/HARCO Bank?** — In 2019 the advertisement was issued by HARCO Bank (for HARCO + 18 DCCBs) and the online exam was conducted by IBPS. Check the method of a new recruitment in its advertisement.
**What was the qualification for clerk?** — In 2019: graduation with 55% or commerce graduation with 50%; age 18-42 years.
**Is there an interview for clerk?** — In the 2019 advertisement there was no interview for clerk; selection was through the online exam.
**Does age relaxation guarantee selection?** — No; it only gives eligibility in terms of age, and that too with the approval of the competent authority.
**Where can I find the conditions of the promotion quota?** — The press release has no conditions; ask your DCCB or RCS office for the amended service rules.

## Three things to remember

- 🏦 Most DCCB clerk posts (70%) are filled by open direct recruitment — a PACS employee can get age relaxation of up to 10 years with the approval of the competent authority.
- 🔼 Approved in 2022: 30% of posts by promotion — 20% for **Class IV** employees of DCCBs and 10% for those of PACS.
- 📄 Take the conditions from each year's advertisement and the service rules — not from hearsay.

## In the end

"Becoming a DCCB clerk from PACS" is not a single route. For most PACS employees it is an open competitive exam, with only the concession of age relaxation; but for Class IV employees of PACS, a 10% promotion quota was also approved in 2022. Identifying the right route is half the preparation — and in every recruitment, treat the official advertisement and the service rules as the final word.
