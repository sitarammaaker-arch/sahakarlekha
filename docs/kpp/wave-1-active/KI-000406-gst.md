# KI-000406 · GST — Goods and Services Tax (माल व सेवा कर)

**Definition:** GST is an indirect tax on the supply of goods and services. A registered society charges GST on its taxable sales (output) and can offset the GST paid on its purchases (input tax credit), depositing the net.

**Plain-language explanation:** On taxable sales the society collects GST from the buyer; on purchases it pays GST to the supplier. It deposits the difference and files returns.

**Hindi explanation (आसान हिंदी):** GST माल व सेवा की आपूर्ति पर लगने वाला अप्रत्यक्ष कर है। पंजीकृत समिति अपनी कर-योग्य बिक्री पर GST वसूलती है (output) और खरीद पर चुकाए GST का श्रेय (ITC) घटाकर शुद्ध राशि जमा करती है। GST लागू होगा या नहीं, यह टर्नओवर व गतिविधि पर निर्भर है।

**English explanation:** Output GST (on sales) minus eligible Input Tax Credit (on purchases) is the net payable, reported in GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B. SahakarLekha prepares the summary; the return is filed on the GST portal.

**Why it matters:** Correct GST on purchases and sales protects input credit and keeps returns accurate. Applicability depends on turnover and activity, not on being a cooperative.

**Common misconceptions:**
- ❌ "Cooperatives are always GST-exempt." → Applicability depends on turnover and activity.
- ❌ "GST on purchases is a cost." → If eligible, it is claimable as input tax credit.
