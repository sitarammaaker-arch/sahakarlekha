# KI-000408 · Deposit (जमा)

**Definition:** A deposit is money placed by a member with the society for safe-keeping and interest — such as Savings (SB), Fixed (FD), Recurring (RD) or Pigmy — which the society owes back to the member.

**Plain-language explanation:** Members keep their savings with the society, which pays interest and returns the money on maturity or withdrawal. To the society, a deposit is a liability (it owes the member).

**Hindi explanation (आसान हिंदी):** सदस्य अपनी बचत समिति में जमा रखते हैं और समिति उस पर ब्याज देती है — बचत (SB), सावधि (FD), आवर्ती (RD) या पिग्मी। समिति के लिए जमा एक देनदारी है (सदस्य का पैसा लौटाना है)। जमा पर नकद/बैंक बढ़ता है और सदस्य का शेष बढ़ता है।

**English explanation:** A deposit debits cash/bank and credits the member's deposit (a liability). Withdrawals, interest and maturity are posted against the same account; the balance is what the society owes the member.

**Why it matters:** Deposits are members' money and a core PACS/credit-society activity. Accurate per-member balances and interest keep members' trust and the books correct.

**Common misconceptions:**
- ❌ "A deposit is the society's income." → It is a liability the society owes back.
- ❌ "SB, FD, RD and Pigmy are recorded the same way." → They differ in interest, maturity and installments.
