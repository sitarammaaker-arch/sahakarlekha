# KI-000404 · Payment Voucher (भुगतान वाउचर)

**Definition:** A payment voucher records money going OUT of the society — cash or bank — such as paying a supplier, an expense, a salary, or a loan disbursement.

**Plain-language explanation:** "Paisa gaya" — whenever the society pays money, it is a payment. The payment voucher notes who was paid, how much, and from cash or bank.

**Hindi explanation (आसान हिंदी):** जब समिति पैसा देती है (आपूर्तिकर्ता को, खर्च, वेतन, कर्ज़ वितरण), तो वह "भुगतान" है। भुगतान वाउचर बताता है — किसे दिया, कितना, और नकद या बैंक से। नकद/बैंक घटता है।

**English explanation:** A payment voucher credits cash/bank and debits the party or expense account. Use "Make Payment" for bill-wise settlement to suppliers.

**Why it matters:** Payments keep the cash/bank book and supplier dues correct, and feed the Receipts & Payments statement. Wrong bank choice breaks bank reconciliation.

**Common misconceptions:**
- ❌ "Payment and receipt are interchangeable." → Payment is money OUT; receipt is money IN.
- ❌ "Bank payment auto-picks the right account." → Choose the correct bank so BRS tallies.
