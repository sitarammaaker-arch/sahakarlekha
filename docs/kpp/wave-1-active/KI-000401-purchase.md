# KI-000401 · Purchase (खरीद)

**Definition:** A purchase is the buying of goods or services by the society — for resale, for members, or for its own use — recorded so that stock and the supplier's account update together.

**Plain-language explanation:** Whenever the society buys something (fertiliser, grocery stock, stationery), that is a purchase. Recording it increases stock and, if bought on credit, shows how much is owed to the supplier.

**Hindi explanation (आसान हिंदी):** जब समिति कोई माल या सेवा खरीदती है (खाद, किराना, सामान), तो वह "खरीद" है। इसे दर्ज करने से स्टॉक बढ़ता है और उधार खरीद हो तो आपूर्तिकर्ता का बकाया दिखता है। नकद खरीद में नकद घटता है।

**English explanation:** A purchase entry debits the stock/expense and credits cash/bank (cash purchase) or the supplier (credit purchase). In SahakarLekha the voucher and stock update automatically.

**Why it matters:** Purchases drive stock valuation, the Trading Account and what the society owes suppliers. Wrong or missing purchase entries throw off closing stock and profit.

**Common misconceptions:**
- ❌ "A separate voucher must be made by hand." → The purchase screen posts the voucher automatically.
- ❌ "Cash and credit purchases are recorded the same way." → Credit purchase creates a supplier due; cash purchase reduces cash immediately.
