# KI-000402 · Sale (बिक्री)

**Definition:** A sale is the selling of goods or services by the society to a member or customer, recorded so that stock reduces and cash or the customer's due updates together.

**Plain-language explanation:** When the society sells something, that is a sale. Recording it reduces stock and either brings in cash or shows how much the customer still owes (credit sale).

**Hindi explanation (आसान हिंदी):** जब समिति कोई माल या सेवा बेचती है, तो वह "बिक्री" है। इसे दर्ज करने से स्टॉक घटता है और नकद बिक्री में नकद बढ़ता है; उधार बिक्री में ग्राहक का बकाया दिखता है। स्टॉक से ज़्यादा बिक्री app रोक देता है।

**English explanation:** A sale entry debits cash/bank (cash sale) or the customer (credit sale) and credits the sales account; stock is reduced. SahakarLekha blocks selling more than the available stock.

**Why it matters:** Sales are the society's income and the basis of the Trading Account. Correct sales entries keep stock, income and customer dues accurate.

**Common misconceptions:**
- ❌ "You can sell more than the stock in hand." → The app prevents negative stock.
- ❌ "Credit sale means the money is already received." → No; it stays a customer due until collected.
