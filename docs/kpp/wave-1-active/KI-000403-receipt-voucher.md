# KI-000403 · Receipt Voucher (रसीद वाउचर)

**Definition:** A receipt voucher records money coming IN to the society — cash or bank — such as a member's payment, a loan repayment, or a customer clearing a bill.

**Plain-language explanation:** "Paisa aaya" — whenever the society receives money, it is a receipt. The receipt voucher notes who paid, how much, and into cash or bank.

**Hindi explanation (आसान हिंदी):** जब समिति को पैसा मिलता है (सदस्य से, कर्ज़ की वापसी, ग्राहक से बकाया), तो वह "रसीद" है। रसीद वाउचर बताता है — किसने दिया, कितना, और नकद या बैंक में। नकद/बैंक बढ़ता है।

**English explanation:** A receipt voucher debits cash/bank and credits the party or income account. Use "Receive Payment" for bill-wise collection from customers.

**Why it matters:** Receipts keep the cash book and members'/customers' dues correct. A missed receipt makes the books show money still owed that was actually paid.

**Common misconceptions:**
- ❌ "Receipt and payment are the same voucher." → Receipt is money IN; payment is money OUT.
- ❌ "You must know debit/credit to record a receipt." → Easy mode hides Dr/Cr — just pick "money in".
