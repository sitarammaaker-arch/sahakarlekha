# KI-000409 · Closing Stock (अंतिम स्टॉक)

**Definition:** Closing stock is the value of unsold goods remaining with the society at the end of a period, carried forward as the opening stock of the next period.

**Plain-language explanation:** Whatever goods are still in the godown/shop at year-end — not yet sold — is the closing stock. Its value affects the profit for the year.

**Hindi explanation (आसान हिंदी):** अवधि के अंत में जो माल बिकने से बचा रहता है, उसका मूल्य "अंतिम स्टॉक" है। यही अगली अवधि का प्रारंभिक स्टॉक बन जाता है। इसका मूल्य साल के मुनाफ़े को प्रभावित करता है। SahakarLekha इसे स्टॉक की आवाजाही (खरीद बढ़ाती, बिक्री घटाती) से अपने-आप निकालता है।

**English explanation:** Closing stock = opening stock + purchases − sales/consumption, valued at cost (or as per policy). It appears in the Trading Account and the Balance Sheet, and is auto-computed from stock movements.

**Why it matters:** Closing stock directly changes gross profit. Wrong stock (or mixing the stock field with movement-based value) creates phantom profit or loss.

**Common misconceptions:**
- ❌ "Closing stock is money in hand." → It is the value of goods, not cash.
- ❌ "It must be typed manually each year." → It is computed from purchases and sales (movements).
