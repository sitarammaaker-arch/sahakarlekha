# KI-000405 · TDS — Tax Deducted at Source (स्रोत पर कर कटौती)

**Definition:** TDS is income tax that the society, while making certain payments (salary, contractor, rent, interest, etc.), deducts at the time of payment and deposits with the government on the payee's behalf.

**Plain-language explanation:** Instead of the receiver paying tax later, the society cuts a small part of the payment as tax and deposits it for them. The receiver gets credit for it.

**Hindi explanation (आसान हिंदी):** कुछ भुगतान करते समय (वेतन, ठेकेदार, किराया, ब्याज) समिति भुगतान में से कर का हिस्सा काटकर सरकार में जमा कर देती है — यही TDS है। जिसका काटा, उसे उस कर का श्रेय (credit) मिलता है। दर व सीमा भुगतान के प्रकार व धारा पर निर्भर करती हैं।

**English explanation:** The society deducts TDS at the applicable rate for the payment type, deposits it by the due date, and reports it in the TDS return (e.g. 24Q/26Q) so the payee can claim credit in Form 26AS / Form 16A.

**Why it matters:** Wrong or missed TDS creates interest, penalty and mismatch for the payee. The society must deduct, deposit and file correctly.

**Common misconceptions:**
- ❌ "TDS is an extra cost to the society." → It is the payee's tax, only routed through the society.
- ❌ "One rate fits all payments." → Rates and thresholds differ by section and payment type — verify each.
